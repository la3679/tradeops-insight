# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration environment. Be constructive, assume good intent while addressing evidence, respect privacy and intellectual property, and accept correction. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or deliberate misuse of the project are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/la3679). Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
