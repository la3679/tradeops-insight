# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in TradeOps Copilot a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, education, appearance, ability, nationality, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing technical or behavioral impact directly.
- Welcome questions and explain decisions without condescension.
- Accept feedback, correct mistakes, and prioritize community safety.
- Protect private, confidential, and security-sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing, sustained disruption, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation that violates these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports will be handled as privately and fairly as practical. Good-faith reports will not be retaliated against.

For conduct concerns, use the private contact method listed on the maintainer's connected GitHub profile. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.
